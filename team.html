Meet the Team

Built by students, for students. The people behind AWM Financial's mission of financial clarity for the next generation.

Founder

Crawford Anderson

Crawford Anderson is the founder of AWM Financial and a finance major at UNC Charlotte. He combines formal education with hands-on experience managing his own investment portfolios, building disciplined saving systems, and applying long-term financial strategy. After seeing how little practical financial education exists for students and young adults, he set out to build a clear, accessible resource for the next generation. Crawford writes the AWM Financial Newsletter and hosts The Core Curriculum Show.
