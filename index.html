About AWM Financial

An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure, and building strong, repeatable habits early is the key to financial freedom. If every high school senior understood that investing just $25 per week starting at graduation could compound to over $1.5 million tax-free by retirement, the next generation would have far more flexibility and security to pursue their goals earlier in life.

Through articles, podcasts, social media, and newsletters, AWM Financial exists to teach the foundational money skills the next generation was never taught, and to help them build the habits that turn small decisions into long-term freedom.
